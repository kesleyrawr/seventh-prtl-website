SP All Throughout the Years

Celebrating the story of Seventh Prtl / Seventh Portal since 2000.

📍 Started in 2000 • Community computer shop journey
History Timeline

From Burnixxx.s.com to Seventh Portal to Seventh Prtl

2000
Started in the summer of year 2000
What began as Burnixxx.s.com later changed its name to Seventh Portal and became a familiar neighborhood computer shop.
15 Years
Fifteen long years of existence
The shop became part of local life — filled with good memories, better days, firsts, heartbreaks, friendships, and shared everyday moments.
Today
The legacy continues
Now known as Seventh Prtl, the business still carries the spirit of Seventh Portal while serving gaming, printing, scanning, school project, and online assistance needs.
“Good memories. Better days. Friends forevs. Heartbreaks, heartaches, firsts. Gain plenty of friends. Diff. generations.”

Credits to: Jan Joseph

People & Community
  • Friends, regulars, different generations, and plenty of people who grew up with the shop.
